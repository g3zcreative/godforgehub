---In the latest installment of the Godforge Gameplay Guide series, content creator **IAmSpeed** sat down for an in-depth interview with **Simon from Fateless Games**. The nearly 40-minute conversation offers a rare, behind-the-scenes look at the development philosophy driving *Godforge* as it moves through its Early Access phase.

From addressing player frustrations to outlining upcoming features, the interview provides critical insights for anyone looking to master the game's mechanics and understand its future trajectory.

## The Strategy Behind Early Access
Unlike many gacha titles that launch with a "finished" build and only minor tweaks, Fateless Games is utilizing Early Access as a genuine collaborative period with the community. Simon emphasized that the current phase is less about marketing and more about **refining the core loop**. 

The developers are closely monitoring how players interact with hero systems and resource management to ensure that the global launch version feels earned and balanced.

## Addressing Community Pain Points
A significant portion of the interview focused on the "pain points" identified by the player base. Simon acknowledged that some early progression hurdles have been more restrictive than intended. Key areas of focus for the development team include:

*   **Resource Scarcity:** Adjusting the economy to ensure players feel rewarded for their time without hitting insurmountable "walls" too early.
*   **UI/UX Improvements:** Streamlining menus and hero management to make the deep tactical elements of the game more accessible.
*   **Hero Balancing:** Ensuring that while rare pulls are powerful, lower-tier heroes remain viable for specific strategic niches.

## Looking Forward: Upcoming Features
While much of the discussion centered on stabilization, Simon teased several exciting developments on the horizon for *Godforge*:

1.  **Enhanced Social Features:** Strengthening the "family" and guild components of the game to foster a more connected community.
2.  **Strategic Depth:** New layers of customization for heroes that will move beyond simple level-ups, allowing players to truly specialize their rosters.
3.  **Refined Endgame:** A focus on making the late-game grind feel meaningful rather than repetitive.

## A Community-First Approach
The takeaway from the interview is clear: Fateless Games is listening. By engaging directly with creators like IAmSpeed and the wider Discord community, the developers are aiming to build *Godforge* into a titan of the genre by prioritizing the player experience over quick monetization.

As Simon noted throughout the talk, "Godforge is rising," and with the feedback gathered during this Early Access period, the game is shaping up to be a tactical RPG powerhouse.
