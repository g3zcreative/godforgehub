---In the latest developer update, Simon (Hellhades) from **Fateless Games** took a deep dive into the current state of *Godforge*. While the team is hard at work polishing the experience, the big takeaway is a shift in focus toward "player agency" and high-end quality-of-life (QoL) features that aim to respect the player's time.

### Release Timeline Update
First, the elephant in the room: the release date. Simon shared that while the game is functionally playable and passed recent tech tests, the team discovered security issues that required immediate attention. As a result, the early access timeline has shifted from February/March to a **March/April** window. 

To make up for the wait and address some early technical glitches, the **Godforge Mini-game has been extended until the end of February**, giving players more time to lock in hero fragments and Aether.

### Revolutionary Quality of Life
Fateless is aiming to fix common gacha pain points with several new systems:

*   **Overflow Experience:** One of the standout features is that max-level heroes no longer "waste" experience. Instead, a portion of the XP earned by maxed characters is converted into a resource you can use to level up other heroes instantly.
*   **No Energy Loss on Failure:** In a major win for strategy enthusiasts, players will **not be charged energy if they fail** a new stage. You are only charged energy upon a successful clearance or when farming. 
*   **Inventory Freedom:** Every player will start with **1,000 inventory slots** from day one—no Ruby unlocks or paywalls required.
*   **Advanced Gear Filtering:** A new "exclusion filter" allows players to quickly find and sell gear that *doesn't* have specific stats (like Speed), making inventory management a breeze.

### Summoning and the "Echo" System
The developers are prioritizing transparency in their summoning mechanics. The UI will now explicitly show exactly how many summons remain before a guaranteed legendary hero (pity/mercy).

Most exciting is the debut of **Echo Summon Fragments**. Every time you summon, you earn these fragments. Once you own a legendary hero, you can use these fragments to "Echo" them—effectively farming duplicates to unlock Imprints or Awakenings without relying purely on luck. This ensures that every hero in your roster can eventually reach max power regardless of your spending level.

### Discord Integration & Collabs
*Godforge* is leaning heavily into social play. The game now features a direct link between **Pantheons (Guilds) and Discord**. Members can chat across both platforms, allowing you to ping your guildmates to jump on for a boss fight or event directly from the game UI.

Finally, keep an eye out for the **Method collaboration**. Fateless has teamed up with the world-renowned WoW raiding guild for an instant giveaway. Head over to the official Discord for a chance to win free games before the event ends on February 14th.
