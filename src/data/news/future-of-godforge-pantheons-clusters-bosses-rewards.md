---The team at Fateless Games recently sat down with community creators Charlemagne and MrSneakyy to pull back the curtain on **Pantheons**, the social cornerstone of Godforge. From membership scaling to custom-built bosses, here is everything we learned about the future of community play.

### Starting Small, Growing Strong: Pantheon Sizes
One of the biggest talking points was the decision to start Pantheons at **20 members**, eventually scaling up to a maximum of **40**. 

Simon Lockerby explained that this "low" starting number is designed to benefit the player experience. High caps can make new Pantheons feel "dead" or impossible to fill for new leaders. By starting at 20, a group can feel active and vital quickly. The team expects a highly active Pantheon to reach the 40-player cap within roughly six months of consistent play.

### The Great Tech Tree Debate
A thrilling prospect discussed was the implementation of a **Pantheon Tech Tree**. Instead of linear upgrades, leaders might have to choose between:
*   **Expansion:** Unlocking more member slots.
*   **Utility:** Boosting Gold or XP gains for all members.
*   **Power:** Enhancing stats specifically for Pantheon-vs-Pantheon encounters.

This introduces a layer of high-level strategy for "Clusters" (groups of multiple Pantheons). Do you focus one Pantheon entirely on recruitment while another focuses on raw combat power? The devs are leaning into "player agency," allowing communities to define their own identity.

### Building Your Own Boss
In a unique twist on the "Clan Boss" trope, the devs revealed plans for Pantheons to essentially **craft their own boss**. Through the Tech Tree, Pantheons could unlock different entities—like a Cyclops or a Chimera—and customize their abilities. 

In future "Pantheon Wars," opposing groups wouldn't just fight a generic NPC; they would face the specific, empowered boss built by their rivals. This "PvE-meets-PvP" approach ensures that even players who prefer fighting AI can contribute to their community’s competitive standing.

### Technical Tidbits: Names, Logos, and Costs
*   **Unlock Level:** Pantheons unlock very early, around Level 5 (roughly 20 minutes into the game).
*   **Creation Cost:** There is a planned cost of **250 Rubies** to create a Pantheon. This is intended to prevent "clan spam" and ensure leaders are making a conscious commitment.
*   **Naming:** Names will be flexible and support multiple words (e.g., "Cabin of Hades"). While the backend account names must be unique, display names will allow for more freedom.
*   **Discord Integration:** Godforge features a deep Discord integration. While you don’t *need* a Discord account to chat in-game (you can join as a "guest"), linking your account allows for seamless communication between the game and your community’s external server.

### What’s Next?
The devs emphasized that Godforge is a "community-led" project. Features like the **Wandering Merchant**—a rare shop that a Pantheon can summon using shared currency—are being designed to create "hype moments" that bring players together.

As Early Access approaches, the team is looking for feedback on these systems. Would you prefer a linear path to 40 members, or do you want the strategic depth of a Tech Tree? Head over to the [Official Godforge Discord](https://discord.com/invite/godforge) to let your voice be heard!
