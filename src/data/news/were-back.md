Hey everyone,

I'm incredibly excited to say: **we are back!**

If you've noticed a lack of updates over the past several months, I want to be completely open with you about why. After building the initial version of Godforge Hub, pouring countless hours into scavenging data, mapping out hero kits, and setting up the database, I looked at the competition and the initial lack of interest, and I hit a wall. I felt burned out, demoralized, and convinced that maybe the demand just wasn't there.

But then, I did something I should have done much sooner—I looked closely at the site's analytics.

## The Turning Point

When I reviewed the analytics during our test periods, the data told a completely different story. The traffic spikes—especially a massive surge in new users around early June—proved that players were actively looking for what we were building. There was a genuine, self-sustaining interest in an easy-to-use, comprehensive database and guide hub for *Godforge*.

Seeing your interest and the actual demand breathed new life into this project. I realized we couldn't just let Godforge Hub gather dust.

## What's New Since March?

Since March, I have quietly been working behind the scenes on a massive overhaul of the entire application. My main goals were to make the platform **easier to maintain** and **completely accurate** to reflect the game's current state.

Here is what has changed:
* **Simplified Codebase & Static Data**: We've transitioned key sections (like the News system you are reading right now!) to a static file structure. This makes it extremely fast to load, easy to update via GitHub, and free from database scaling hiccups.
* **Up-to-Date Hero Database**: Every single hero profile, element, class, skill, scaling formula, and awakening bonus has been reviewed, scrubbed, and updated to reflect the absolute latest game patch.
* **Accuracy Adjustments**: Fixed math discrepancies in scaling formulas and updated archetypes/faction configurations to match the latest game client data.

## Built for the Long Haul

This overhaul isn't just a quick patch—it's a restructuring designed to make Godforge Hub a **long-lasting, reliable source** for easy-to-understand Godforge information, guides, tips, and tricks. By reducing the maintenance overhead, we can focus on writing high-quality strategy guides, team-building tools, and tier lists instead of constantly fighting code complexity.

Thank you so much to everyone who visited the site, left feedback, or used our tools during the early tests. Your interest is the reason we're back, and we can't wait to forge ahead with you.

Stay tuned for more guides and features coming very soon!

— **Xsunami**
