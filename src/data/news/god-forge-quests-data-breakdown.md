---The community-favorite mini-game, **God Forge Quests**, is officially drawing to a close. To mark the occasion, lead developer **Fateless** has pulled back the curtain on the backend data, offering players a fascinating look at how the community interacted with the event. 

From massive summon numbers to the health of the in-game economy, here is the breakdown of the most important statistics from the God Forge Quests era.

## Summon Stats and Hero Popularity
The sheer volume of engagement during this event surpassed developer expectations. Millions of summons were performed during the quest window, but what caught the team's eye was *who* players were chasing. 

While the meta-defining Legendary heroes saw high participation, a surprising rise in the popularity of mid-tier Elemental units was noted. Fateless indicated that this trend suggests players are focusing more on synergistic team builds rather than just "raw power" summons.

## The Aether Economy: A Surplus of Wealth?
One of the most critical metrics for any gacha game is its economy. Fateless provided a deep dive into the **Aether** (premium currency) flow during the event. 

Contrary to concerns of inflation, the Aether economy remained remarkably stable. While the mini-game distributed a significant amount of currency through milestone rewards, the "burn rate" (Aether spent on upgrades and limited banners) kept pace. This balance ensures that the game’s long-term sustainability remains intact as we transition into the next major content patch.

## Surprising Player Retention
Perhaps the most encouraging news for the Godforge community is the retention rate. God Forge Quests saw a **25% increase in daily active users** compared to the previous quarter. 

Fateless noted that the "bite-sized" nature of the quests encouraged players who usually log in once a week to participate daily. This high retention rate is a strong signal to the development team that mini-games and interactive side-content will likely become a permanent fixture in the Godforge roadmap.

## What’s Next?
As God Forge Quests ends, the rewards collected will be vital for the upcoming seasonal transition. Fateless teased that the data gathered here—specifically regarding player behavior in the Aether market—will directly influence the reward structures for the next major event.
