### From Discord to Devs: Your Beta Feedback and Gameplay Debates Drive Godforge Forward!

Greetings, Godforgers!

The forge of ideas is always blazing in our Discord channels, and this past week was no exception! We've seen incredible engagement, deep dives into crucial gameplay mechanics, and invaluable feedback from our recent beta. Your passion and detailed insights are truly instrumental in shaping Godforge into the best game it can be. Let's recap some of the standout discussions!

#### Gameplay Deep Dive: The Intercept & Retaliate Conundrum

A significant portion of the #gameplay-discussion channel focused on the intricate interaction between the **Retaliate** and **Intercept** mechanics, particularly concerning Heimdall's kit. Players have been debating whether a Retaliate effect should proc if a character is targeted by an attack but that damage is fully nullified by Intercept.

*   **The Core Debate:** Many players feel that being *targeted* by an attack should be sufficient to trigger Retaliate, regardless of whether damage was successfully dealt. They argue that the intent of the attack was there, and a retaliatory strike makes logical sense within the game's fantasy, promoting more dynamic counter-play.
*   **Heimdall's Intercept Under the Microscope:** The discussion naturally led to Heimdall's Intercept ability. Several players recalled its behavior in the Alpha phase, where it specifically intercepted an attack on a *single targeted unit*. The current Beta implementation, which sees Heimdall intercepting *all* AoE attacks, has sparked calls for a re-evaluation.
*   **Community Suggestions:**
    *   **Revert Intercept:** A common suggestion is to revert Heimdall's Intercept back to its Alpha functionality, protecting only the specifically targeted unit rather than an entire AoE.
    *   **Kit Rework/Buff Separation:** Others proposed a full rework of Heimdall's kit to prevent Intercept and Retaliate from being on the same skill or from having conflicting interactions, ensuring clear player agency and build paths.
    *   **Clarify Interaction:** Ensuring that if these abilities are to exist together, their interaction is clear, intuitive, and doesn't lead to "dead" builds where players feel their strategic choices are undermined.

This robust discussion highlights the community's desire for consistent mechanics that empower strategic team building and champion design. We're actively reviewing these insights!

#### Comprehensive Beta Feedback: Shaping the Godforge Experience

Beyond specific mechanics, we received a fantastic, detailed compilation of feedback from a dedicated player regarding their experience during the first Beta, both on mobile and PC. This kind of comprehensive review is incredibly valuable, and we're grateful for the time and effort invested.

Here’s a summary of the key points raised:

*   **Mobile Experience (iPhone):**
    *   **Screen Rotation:** A desire for unlocked screen rotation to allow for flexible device orientation.
    *   **Sound Settings:** The game not remembering previous sound settings, requiring manual adjustment upon each launch.
*   **PC Experience (Windows 11):**
    *   **ESC Key Navigation:** Request for the Esc key to consistently function as a "back" or "close current menu" button when not in the Sanctuary, mirroring the 'X' button functionality.
*   **General Gameplay & Aesthetics:**
    *   **Quetzalcoatl Animation:** Feedback on Quetzalcoatl's post-battle animation, specifically its "screaming" effect. Suggestions included varying the post-battle animation to feature a random champion or the one delivering the final blow, or simply refining Quetzalcoatl's animation.
    *   **Alchemy Stone Consistency:** A desire for greater visual consistency in Alchemy stones, specifically regarding their leaning direction and spike orientation.

We want to extend a massive thank you to everyone who participated in the beta and took the time to provide such thorough feedback. Each point helps us refine the game, ensuring a smoother, more enjoyable experience across all platforms.
