---In the latest deep dive from the Fateless Games headquarters, founders Simon Lockerby (Hellhades), Dan Francis (Phixion), and Hisham Saleh (Sham) sat down with industry veteran Zdravko to pull back the curtain on the development of **Godforge**. 

The discussion provides a candid look at the friction between high-level creative vision and the technical realities of building a next-generation gacha RPG. For the GodforgeHub community, these insights offer a roadmap of what to expect as we approach the highly anticipated Early Access phase.

### Scaling Teams and Managing Roles
A significant portion of the conversation focused on the internal structure of Fateless Games. Transitioning from a small core team to a full-scale production environment requires a delicate balance of roles. The team distinguished between Project Managers and Producers—clarifying that while one focuses on the "how" and "when," the other ensures the creative "soul" of the game remains intact.

Zdravko shared his experience on how scaling too quickly can often lead to "feature creep," emphasizing that for Godforge, the focus is on a tight, high-quality core experience before expanding.

### The Power of the MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
For players eager to get their hands on the game, the developers discussed the importance of the **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**. The philosophy at Fateless is not to release a "bare-bones" game, but rather a polished, functional foundation. 

By focusing on the MVP for the Early Access launch:
*   **Balance can be perfected:** Core combat and hero kits will be the primary focus.
*   **Technical Stability:** Ensuring account systems and UI/UX are seamless before adding complex layers.
*   **Iterative Design:** Using community feedback to shape the game's evolution rather than guessing what players want.

### Building Community Trust Through Transparency
One of the most poignant moments of the video addressed the fragile nature of community trust. The founders acknowledged that in the live-service and hero-collector space, players are often wary of predatory systems or broken promises.

Fateless Games aims to combat this by maintaining an open dialogue throughout the development process. "The Truth About Early Access," as the title suggests, is that it won't be perfect on day one—but the team is committed to making it a collaborative journey with the players.

### Design Focus: Enhancing User Experience
The devs also touched upon common frustrations in the genre, specifically poor account systems and clunky UIs. The goal for Godforge is to create a streamlined experience where players spend more time strategizing and less time navigating menus. This includes a more intuitive approach to hero management and "Svarog's Hoard," which aims to innovate on the classic gear-grind loop.

### What’s Next for Godforge?
As the team moves closer to the Early Access milestone, fans are encouraged to sign up on the [official Fateless website](https://fateless.gg/). By participating in the early stages, players aren't just testing the game—they're helping the dev team bridge the gap between technical limitations and the epic gacha vision we’re all waiting for.
