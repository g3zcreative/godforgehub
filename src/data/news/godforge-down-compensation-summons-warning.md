---The **Godforge** community has hit a significant roadblock this week as the main website and the companion app, **Godforge Go**, have gone offline. As of this writing, services have been inaccessible for over 24 hours, leaving many players unable to manage their accounts or access web-based features.

The development team at **Fateless** has acknowledged the ongoing technical difficulties. While they haven't provided a specific timeline for when full service will be restored, they have taken several immediate steps to appease the community and mitigate the impact of the downtime.

### Massive Compensation Package
In a surprising move to compensate for the frustration, Fateless has pushed out a hefty rewards package to all players. Upon logging back into the game client (which remains partially accessible for many), players will find:

*   **250 Free Summons**
*   **5,000 Aether**
*   **Removal of the 20 Fragment Cost:** The controversial fragment cost for specific upgrades has been completely removed as part of this update.

### A Warning to Summoners
Despite the influx of free summons, the developers have issued a **strong recommendation: do not use your summons yet.** 

Fateless warns that due to the instability of the backend servers and the website outage, summon results may not sync correctly with accounts. To avoid the risk of losing rare pulls or encountering "rollback" issues, players are advised to hold onto their tickets until an official "all clear" is given via the game’s social channels.

### Minor Updates
While the team works on the server architecture, a few small cosmetic additions have made it through. Players can now find a couple of **new character portraits** available in the profile selection menu to freshen up their in-game look while they wait for the servers to stabilize.

### Looking Ahead
Currently, there are no new updates regarding exactly when the website or Godforge Go will return to full functionality. We recommend following the official Discord for real-time alerts.

We will keep you updated as more information becomes available. In the meantime, enjoy the freebies, but stay away from the summon gate!
