Welcome, Forgemasters!

With the first official Beta testing phase for **Godforge** having concluded on June 2, 2026, the team at Fateless Games has been working tirelessly to compile and analyze the wealth of feedback and data gathered during this massive milestone. 

Today, we are thrilled to share some incredible highlights from Beta 1, what we are working on behind the scenes, and what you can expect as we forge the path toward Beta 2!

---

### Beta 1 by the Numbers

The enthusiasm from our community during Beta 1 completely shattered our expectations. Here are the key statistics from the test:

*   **75,000 Active Players:** Approximately 75,000 unique players stepped into the forge, helping us test matchmaking, game servers, and game balance at scale.
*   **64% Mobile / 36% Desktop Split:** The majority of our players accessed the game via mobile devices (iOS TestFlight and Google Play Store). This split has made **mobile optimization** a top-tier priority for our engineering team. We are actively working to improve device compatibility, reduce battery consumption, and fix memory crashes on older phone models.
*   **Vibrant Feedback Loop:** Over 6,000 players completed our Beta End Survey. This is an incredible response rate for the gaming industry, providing our product team with invaluable data.

---

### Key Areas of Focus & Feedback

Our game designers and developers are currently focusing on several core systems based directly on your reports:

1.  **Imprint & Weapon Flexibility:** We've heard your feedback regarding weapon customisation and the desire for more flexible Imprint re-allocation. The team is exploring concepts like extracting Imprints via rare progression resources or overlaying weapons in the Alchemy tab to avoid early-game resource lock-in.
2.  **Combat & UI/UX Refinement:** We are working on polishing turn transitions, enhancing the clarity of status effects, and ensuring skill scaling formulas are easy to understand directly in-game.
3.  **Client Optimization & Stability:** Building on the hotfixes deployed during the beta (such as resolving the Hercules infinite turn loop and stability updates), we are focusing heavily on memory management and frame rates across both PC/Mac and mobile platforms.

---

### What Carries Over to Beta 2?

As a reminder, the game servers are currently in **Maintenance mode** while we prepare the next version. 

*   **Hero Carry-Over:** All heroes you unlocked during Beta 1 (including Ra, King Arthur, or Hades obtained via Echo Summons) **will carry over** to Beta 2!
*   **Reset Details:** These carried-over heroes will be reset to **Level 1** and will not retain awakenings. Please note that while heroes carry over between beta tests to allow for further gameplay testing, they will not carry over into the official Early Access launch.
*   **Account Safety:** Make sure you save your Fateless ID login credentials. You will need them to log back into your account and retrieve your heroes when Beta 2 goes live.

---

### Looking Ahead: Beta 2 and New Features

While we do not have a confirmed date for Beta 2 just yet, we want to make sure we take the time to get it right. When the servers light up again, players can look forward to testing several major additions, including:
*   **Pantheon Guild Boss content**
*   **The Tower progression system**
*   **Initial PvP Arena testing**

Thank you, Forgemasters, for your incredible support, patience, and dedication. You are helping us build a turn-based RPG worthy of the gods!

Stay tuned to our website and the official Discord channel for the announcement of the Beta 2 schedule.
