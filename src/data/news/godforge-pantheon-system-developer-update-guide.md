---The developers of **Godforge** have finally pulled back the curtain on the **Pantheon system**, a comprehensive social platform designed to foster community, cooperation, and high-level competition. As we move closer to the next phase of Early Access, the Pantheon system is set to become the central pillar of the endgame experience.

Here is everything we know about how you and your allies will conquer the heavens together.

---

## Building Your Divine Council
At its core, a Pantheon is Godforge’s version of a guild or clan. Upon launch, each Pantheon will have a **strict member limit** to ensure tight-knit communities rather than massive, unmanageable cohorts. 

While the starting capacity is modest, it can be expanded through dedicated play. The developers have hinted that managing your roster will be crucial for the collaborative challenges ahead.

## Leveling Your Pantheon
A Pantheon is a living entity that grows with its members. By completing daily missions, participating in world events, and donating specific resources, members contribute to their **Pantheon Level**.

**Higher levels unlock:**
*   **Passive Stat Buffs:** Small but significant boosts to Attack, HP, or Crit Rate for all members.
*   **The Pantheon Shop:** A unique storefront where specialized currency earned through guild activities can be traded for rare ascension materials and exclusive Hero Shards.
*   **Expanded Ranks:** Unlock more officer slots to help manage your growing community.

## Collaborative Boss Battles: The Titan Raids
The most anticipated feature of the system is the **Pantheon Boss Battle**. Unlike standard campaign bosses, these "Titans" possess massive health pools that require an entire Pantheon to deplete over several days.

*   **Coordinated Strikes:** Members get a set number of attempts per day to deal as much damage as possible.
*   **Scaling Difficulty:** As your Pantheon defeats a Titan, the next one will emerge stronger, offering better rewards.
*   **Shared Loot:** Rewards aren't just for the top damage dealers. While there are "MVP" bonuses, every contributing member receives a share of the spoils, encouraging even newer players to participate.

## Looking Toward the Future
During the initial Early Access rollout, the developers will be monitoring the Pantheon system closely for balance. Future updates are expected to introduce **Pantheon vs. Pantheon (PvP)** wars and custom **Pantheon Halls**—a customizable social space where members can hang out and display their collective trophies.
