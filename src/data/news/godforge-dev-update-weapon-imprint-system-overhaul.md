---In the latest development update from Fateless Games, Simon Lockerby (better known as Hellhades) pulled back the curtain on some significant overhauls coming to **Godforge**. With "feature lock" less than a month away, the team is refining the game's core systems, specifically focusing on how players interact with Weapons and Imprints.

### A New Era for the Smithy
The Smithy has received a massive visual and functional upgrade. Fateless is leaning into the idea that a weapon should feel as significant as a hero. To achieve this, several changes are being implemented:

*   **Rarity Focus:** In the upcoming Early Access build, the game will focus on **Epic and Legendary weapons**. These have been enhanced to feel truly "game-changing" compared to their Alpha iterations.
*   **Visual Fidelity:** Weapons are being treated like hero models. Players will soon be able to zoom in and rotating them under improved lighting to appreciate the detail and craftsmanship.
*   **Streamlined Resources:** Based on Alpha feedback, the team has consolidated the enhancement process. Moving forward, a single primary resource will be used to level up weapons, reducing inventory clutter. Players can also recoup these resources by selling old gear.
*   **Refinement and Stats:** The stat possibilities for weapons have been tweaked to offer more value. The refinement system remains, requiring duplicate weapons to boost unique passive effects—such as increasing barrier gains or dealing massive execution damage to low-HP enemies.

### The Power of Imprints
The Imprint system remains one of Godforge's most innovative features, allowing players to slot powerful, build-defining abilities into their weapons. 

The update introduces a revamped **Imprint Library** featuring a "ghostly" hero UI. Fateless intends for Imprints to be "premium rewards," found at the top tier of event rewards or as high-end shop rotations. While the **Extraction** system is currently being finalized, future features like **Transmutation** and **Schematics** were teased as post-MVP (Minimum Viable Product) additions to give players more ways to craft their perfect loadouts.

### Personalizing Your Portfolio: The Profile Overhaul
Beyond combat, the player profile screen is seeing a complete redesign. This isn't just a static menu; it’s a hub for personal expression within the Godforge community. New features include:
*   **Showcase Heroes:** Select your top five favorite heroes to display on your profile.
*   **Customizable Aesthetics:** Earn and swap out portraits, frames, and background banners through gameplay achievements.
*   **Player Titles:** New unlockable "labels" or archetypes will allow you to show off your playstyle or milestones.

### Technical Polish
Simon also touched on the technical side, noting that the game is being heavily optimized for mobile devices. This includes automatic graphic setting detection based on phone hardware and full localization support across multiple languages.

With the game inching closer to its Early Access launch, these updates suggest a high level of polish and a commitment to deep, rewarding progression systems.
